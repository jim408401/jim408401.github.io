About Me

I'm Jim Wu — a software engineer at e-SOFT, working across backend, full-stack and AI application development for enterprise systems and cybersecurity products.

I also rewrote a single-threaded import to run concurrently — 5,000 records a second became 11,000. Before that I interned at Delta Electronics, building a RAG knowledge base with LangChain, Ollama and ChromaDB, and a network topology visualization with D3.js.
